Scientific Methodology & Algorithmic Rigor

ForenSight is engineered exclusively using classical computer vision, digital signal processing, and cryptographic standards. It deliberately avoids opaque neural networks, black-box deep learning, or unverified generative detection APIs.

Documented Algorithmic Modules

  • Metadata & Quantization Tables: ISO 12234-2 EXIF extraction, editor software flags, timestamp desynchronization, and JPEG DQT parsing.
  • Error Level Analysis (ELA): Krawetz (2007) resaved differential analysis at Q=90 with regional mean + 2.5σ anomaly localization.
  • 2D Fourier Analysis (FFT): Popescu & Farid (2005) Hann-windowed log spectrum S(u,v) = log(1 + |F(u,v)|) with conjugate peak clustering.
  • Resampling Spectral Correlation: Li et al. (2026, PubMed: 42574416) 2nd-derivative discrete difference marginal power spectrum correlation.
  • Copy-Move Duplication: Amerini et al. (2011) SIFT keypoints with Lowe's ratio test, spatial distance exclusion (≥30px), and RANSAC homography.
  • Noise & Residual Dispersion: Lyu et al. (2014) high-pass Laplacian residuals with patch-level Median Absolute Deviation (MAD / 0.6745) z-scores.
  • Edge & Boundary Discontinuities: Canny edge density and Sobel gradient magnitude localization along candidate splicing seams.
  • C2PA Provenance: C2PA Specification v1.3 JUMBF manifest extraction and cryptographic asset binding verification via c2patool.
